How a project runs

Most websites go live within one to three weeks. Web apps are planned in stages.

  1. Discover

    A call about your goals, who will use the site and what it must do. You get a written quote with a fixed scope.

  2. Design

    Layouts and the look, shown on your phone and laptop before any code is written.

  3. Build

    I build on a private preview link you can open any time, and we adjust as we go.

  4. Launch

    Domain connected, staff shown how to use the admin, and support after it goes live.

What sets the price

  • Number of pages and features, from a single landing page to a full portal.
  • An admin area so your staff can update news, courses or products.
  • Logins, payments and reports, such as M-Pesa checkout or role-based access.
  • Design work, including logos, posters and brand guides.
  • Timeline, if you need it live sooner than usual.

Domain and hosting are billed separately at cost. Monthly support for updates, backups and fixes is available after launch.

Questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what the site needs to do. Simple landing pages start from KES 15,000. Send a few details through the form or WhatsApp and you'll get a written quote, usually within a day.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes three to seven days. A full business or school website takes one to three weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come in.

Can my staff update the site themselves?

Yes. Business and school websites come with an admin area for news, events, courses or products, and I show your team how to use it.

Can you take M-Pesa payments on my site?

Yes. I integrate paybill and till payments so customers pay from their phones and you see each payment recorded in your dashboard.

Can you improve a website I already have?

Yes. I can redesign it, speed it up, move it off WordPress or fix what's broken. Send me the link and I'll tell you what I'd change.

Do you still do posters and flyers?

Yes, both on their own and alongside a website project.
